Services

Cleaning plans built around your home, not a package tier.

Every visit follows the same room-by-room checklist. Tell us your priorities during booking — pets, allergies, a room to skip — and it carries over to every future clean.

01

Standard Clean

Weekly · biweekly · monthly

The regular upkeep clean, for homes already in good shape that just need consistent maintenance.

  • Kitchen counters, sink, stovetop, and exterior of appliances
  • Full bathroom clean: toilets, showers, tubs, sinks, mirrors
  • Dusting of reachable surfaces, floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Beds made, trash emptied, general tidy-through

02

Deep Clean

Recommended for first visits

Everything in a Standard Clean, plus the buildup that only gets addressed a few times a year.

  • Baseboards, door frames, and light switch plates
  • Interior of the microwave and exterior of major appliances, detailed
  • Window sills and tracks, ceiling fans, and light fixtures
  • Grout and tile detailing in kitchens and bathrooms
Get a free quote for this →
03

Move-In / Move-Out Clean

Empty homes, top to bottom

A full reset for an empty or nearly empty home — before you move in, after you move out, or ahead of a walkthrough.

  • Inside and outside of all cabinets, drawers, and closets
  • Inside of the oven, fridge, and other major appliances
  • All baseboards, vents, and switch plates
  • Every room detailed floor to ceiling, ready for the next set of boxes

04

Recurring Maintenance

Set a schedule, skip when you need to

A Standard Clean on a set cadence, so the deep-clean effort only has to happen once. Priced lower per visit than one-time bookings.

  • Choose weekly, biweekly, or monthly visits
  • Same cleaner and checklist each time when possible
  • Reschedule or skip a visit with advance notice, no penalty
